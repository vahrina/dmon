what is this?

dmon is a minimal fork of the unfortunately soon defunct myrient with following features

  • only a portion of what myrient originally provided (> 390tb)
  • no advertisements
  • no registration
  • no paywalls

is this all that's left?

no! luckily, many people are actively contributing to preseve digital history ⸜(｡˃ ᵕ ˂)⸝♡
